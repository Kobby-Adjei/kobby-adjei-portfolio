Why these two sit together

A design system and a model benchmark look like different disciplines. They are the same move made twice.

In both, the hard part is not the building. It is getting a fuzzy judgement — this feels cheap, this clip is good — into a form precise enough that someone else can apply it, disagree with it, or prove it wrong. Principles do that for design. A rubric and a blind protocol do it for a model.

Once taste is written down, two useful things follow. You can hold quality steady while changing everything underneath it — which is how the model work cut inference cost by roughly three quarters without the output getting worse. And you can tell the difference between a real regression and someone simply not liking it today.
